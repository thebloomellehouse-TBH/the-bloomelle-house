Home beauty service vs parlour visit: which is better?

Compare convenience, comfort and time when choosing between a home beauty service and a parlour in Jhansi.

  • Time: a home visit removes travel and waiting, especially on wedding mornings.
  • Comfort: you stay in your own space, with family around.
  • Group bookings: one artist can serve the whole family at your home.
  • Outfit safety: you do not have to travel in full makeup or a heavy outfit.
  • Choose a professional who brings a clean, organised setup.
