Pre-bridal care checklist: what to do before the wedding

A simple timeline for skin, hair and nail care before your wedding, with advice to start early.

  • Start skin and hair care early, so results are gradual and natural.
  • Keep your routine simple and avoid trying new products right before the wedding.
  • Plan nails and grooming a few days before the event.
  • Drink water and sleep well in the week of the wedding.
  • Share your date with your beauty professional so the plan fits your schedule.
