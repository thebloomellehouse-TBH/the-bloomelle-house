How to choose a bridal makeup artist in Jhansi

Checklist for brides in Jhansi: what to ask, what to check and when to book your bridal makeup artist.

  • Look at real work: ask for photos and videos of past bridal looks, not only edited posts.
  • Ask for a trial so you can see how the makeup suits your skin and outfit.
  • Confirm what is included: makeup, hair, draping, touch-ups and travel.
  • Check the timing: your artist should plan the schedule around your muhurat.
  • Book early, as wedding-season dates fill fast.
