Your skin, handled with care

Please tell us before your visit about allergies, sensitive skin, medicines you use on your face, and any recent procedures or sunburn. This helps us choose safe products.

If you have active or severe skin problems, please consult a dermatologist first. Facials are for skin care and glow, not medical treatment.

  • Share allergies and skin concerns when you book
  • Avoid new treatments in the last week before an event
  • Skip waxing and threading on your face on the same day
  • Drink water and arrive without heavy makeup

Facial FAQs

Can I get a facial at home in Jhansi?

Yes. We bring a trained professional, a clean setup and professional products to your home in Jhansi and nearby areas.

How do I know which facial suits my skin?

Message us your skin type and any concerns. We suggest a treatment before you book, and our professional checks your skin again at your visit.

When should a bride start facials before the wedding?

Ideally 6 to 8 weeks before, with a planned set of visits. Avoid trying any new treatment in the last week.

Is a facial safe if I have sensitive or acne-prone skin?

Tell us about your skin and any allergies when you book. We choose gentle products and avoid anything that may irritate. For active or severe skin conditions, please consult a dermatologist first.

How long does a facial take?

A cleanup usually takes about 45 minutes and a full facial about 60 to 90 minutes. We confirm timing when you book.
