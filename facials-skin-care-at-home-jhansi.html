Aftercare for lasting glow

A few simple steps help your skin look its best for longer.

  • Avoid direct sun and use sunscreen the next day
  • Skip scrubs and harsh products for 24 hours
  • Keep makeup light for the first few hours
  • Drink plenty of water and moisturise daily
  • Avoid touching or picking at your skin

Facial FAQs

Do you do facials at my home in Jhansi?

Yes. Our professional comes to your doorstep in Jhansi and nearby areas. Message us if you are not sure we cover your area.

Which facial is right for me?

We check your skin type and concerns first, then recommend the best option. You can also tell us the occasion.

When should I get a facial before my wedding?

Message us as soon as your wedding dates are fixed. We usually plan a facial a few days to a couple of weeks ahead, and can add it to a pre-bridal package.

How long does a facial take?

A clean-up is quicker, while a full facial takes longer. We will give you an estimate when we confirm.

I have sensitive skin or allergies. What should I do?

Please tell us before the appointment so we can choose suitable products. If you have a skin condition, check with your doctor first.

What are your working hours?

9 AM to 6 PM, Monday to Saturday. Message us any time and we will reply during working hours.
